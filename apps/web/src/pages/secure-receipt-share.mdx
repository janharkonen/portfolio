# Secure receipt share

![Secure receipt share](/projects/secure-receipt-share-2026.webp)

## Overview

Secure receipt share is a simple way to share receipts with colleagues without dumping sensitive files into random chat threads. The app focuses on controlled sharing: upload a receipt, share access intentionally, and keep the flow lightweight enough that people actually use it.

It was a practical internal-tool style product with auth and a real-time backend from day one.

## Key Features

- Upload and share receipts securely
- Authenticated access for colleagues
- Lightweight sharing workflow
- Real-time backend for document state
