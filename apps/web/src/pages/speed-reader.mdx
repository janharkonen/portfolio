# Speed reader

![Speed reader](/projects/speed-reader-2026.webp)

## Overview

Speed reader is a lightweight .epub ebook reader with RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation) capability. Instead of scanning whole pages, you can flash words in sequence at a controlled pace — useful for focused reading sessions and for experimenting with reading speed.

I kept the surface area small: open an epub, read normally or switch into RSVP mode, and stay out of the way of the text.

## Key Features

- Lightweight .epub reading
- RSVP mode for paced word-by-word reading
- Minimal browser UI
- Simple hosting on Vercel
